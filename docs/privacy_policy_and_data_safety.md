### 1. Information We Collect

We collect limited personal information strictly necessary to calculate Vedic astrology Kundli charts and deliver AI consultations:

1. **User Profile Information**:
   - **Google Account ID & Email**: Used for secure account authentication, cloud synchronization, and purchase history recovery.
   - **Name, Birth Date, Birth Time, and Birth Place**: Used exclusively to compute your Vedic Kundli (Lagna, Moon Sign, Nakshatra, Dasha, and House placements).
2. **Consultation Logs**:
   - **AI Chat Messages**: Questions submitted to Devi Priya AI Astrologer to generate personalized readings.
3. **Financial & Purchase Data**:
   - **Google Play Purchase Tokens & Order IDs**: Processed securely via Google Play Billing API for digital subscriptions and credit packs. *We never collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI PINs.*

### 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Astrological Calculations**: To compute sidereal planet longitudes, Lagna cusps, and Vimshottari Dashas.
- **AI Consultation Context**: To provide personalized, Kundli-aware AI astrologer guidance.
- **Account Synchronization**: To allow users to restore purchases and access saved birth charts across devices.

### 3. Data Protection & Sharing

- **Zero Sale of Data**: We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or birth charts with third-party data brokers or advertisers.
- **Encryption in Transit**: All data exchanged between the app and server is encrypted using HTTPS / SSL (TLS 1.3).
- **Trusted Service Providers**:
  - **Google Firebase**: Cloud authentication and Firestore database storage.
  - **Google Play Billing**: Payment processing.
  - **Google Gemini API**: Generative AI text processing.

---

### 4. Account & Data Deletion Rights (GDPR & Google Play Policy)

You have full control over your data. You can request permanent account and data deletion at any time:
1. Open the app $\rightarrow$ Go to **Profile & Privacy**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Confirming deletion will permanently purge your Google sign-in session, all saved birth profiles, consultation history, and cloud user records.

Alternatively, you can email a deletion request to `support@jyotishai.com` with your Google Account email.
